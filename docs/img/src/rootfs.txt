title | A microVM cell: one snapshot, then a clone for each cell, its disk a reflink
sub | make fc-snapshot takes the snapshot once; the pool's create clones it for every cell it builds
desc | Left, make fc-snapshot, once. It copies the image's disk to golden.ext4 and boots a microVM from it, with the
desc | cell brought to warm inside. It freezes the cell's containers with docker pause unless FREEZE=0, pauses the
desc | microVM and writes a full snapshot, vm.snap and vm.mem. Right, the pool's create, for each cell. It reflinks
desc | golden.ext4 into a disk of its own that shares its blocks, and makes network and mount namespaces, under the
desc | jailer by default. A veth pair to the host, tap0 and a DNAT let every clone keep one guest address. The cell's
desc | disk is bound at the path the snapshot recorded, in its mount namespace only. Then snapshot/load, and resume once
desc | the VM holds the cell's disk, not golden.ext4. The host sets the guest clock over one ssh session, and the
desc | containers thaw only after the set. Then the pool's health check passes, and the cell is warm. A snapshot taken
desc | with FREEZE=0 is not frozen, and no thaw runs. make fc-restore runs the same restore on one microVM on the host's
desc | own network, with no pool, and times it to warm.
